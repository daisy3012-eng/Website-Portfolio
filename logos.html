Anakàlypsi Christian Montessori

Anakàlypsi Christian Montessori is a distinctive Montessori school based in Bangalore. Anakàlypsi, meaning “Discovery” in Greek, is built on the belief that every child is unique and precious. The inspiration behind the identity came from a simple question a child asked: “How can God make us all different?”

Through a conversation inspired by Theology, the answer unfolded in the form of thumbprints - a reminder that no two individuals are alike. This idea of individuality became the foundation of the brand identity. The logo incorporates the uniqueness of fingerprints, while playful elements and a vibrant colour palette reflect the school’s interactive, nurturing approach to learning.

Nakshatra

Nakshatra Coffee is a Swedish coffee brand founded by Johann Nishant, a software architect by profession, astro-photographer by passion, who has a vision to put Indian Speciality Coffee on the world coffee stage.

Johann is from South India, and currently lives in Göteborg, Sweden. Passionate about the stars, galaxies and coffee, my challenge was to incorporate the two into a logo unit and identity.

You Can Dance

You Can Dance! (YCD) is a dance wellness brand situated in New Delhi. The idea was to bring out the fun, wellness aspect for the brand. I used an enclosed circle in the logo to visually keep it as a unit. The plum colour denotes richness and boldness. The lotus denotes the wellness aspect of the brand where people can make a connection.

In the future, the brand intends to expand to Dance Bars, Vegan Cafe’s. Keping that in mind, the ‘Dance Wellness’ text part of the logo has the opportunity to change into other collateral like “Vegan Organic Cafe”, “Dance Bars” etc, while retaining the same logo unit.
