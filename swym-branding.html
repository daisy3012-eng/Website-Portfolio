Brand Refresh for Swym

As the lead designer for Swym's website relaunch, I prioritized revitalizing their visual identity by emphasizing their signature green and refining the layout for clarity and rhythm. The result is a sleek, modern, and cohesive design that not only elevates the user experience but also remains deeply aligned with Swym's core values.

I redesigned six core pages: the Homepage, Wishlist Plus Feature, Capture Shoppers Data, Pricing, Partner, and Case Study pages. Additionally, I developed adaptable templates for the Partner Integration and Case Study pages, streamlining future updates and ensuring consistency across the site.

By centering the design around Swym’s signature green and crafting a user experience that feels both fresh and familiar, the website now delivers a visually striking design while reflecting Swym’s unwavering commitment to growth and innovation. This project was developed as a pitch and is currently in progress—yet to be launched by the company.
